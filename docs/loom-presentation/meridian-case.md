# Meridian Trust: fictional Open Finance intervention

Meridian Trust is an invented institution for this website illustration. Its legal status, research, decisions, setup tasks and release blockers are authored scenario content, not a customer engagement or outputs of an executed Loom run. No real names, records, accounts, credentials or payments are used.

## Narrative and artifacts

- M01: establish sponsor interest, customer problem and competing interventions. Authorize discovery only.
- M02: compare PFM alone with PFM plus payment initiation; establish economics, feasibility, authority and stop conditions. The worked branch permits synthetic exploration while regulatory scope remains unresolved.
- M03: model account-data consent separately from payment authorization; prototype through simulated Hub and bank services. Define evidence for stale data, repeated submissions and unknown payment outcomes.
- M04: withhold production authorization because reconciliation evidence and the regulatory route remain unresolved. These are illustrative blockers, not findings from a real execution.
- M05: describe hypothetical monitoring after a separately authorized launch, and a simulated outcome review that feeds a new intervention. The blocked scenario does not silently become a live service.

The three toolkit excerpts use Loom's intake handoff, configuration inventory and evidence-example structures at the existing pinned AI-DLC commit. Meridian's values and roles are editorial additions, not copied repository results. The repository's separate deliberately refusable release example retains its original provenance.

## Domain grounding

Applied the local `open-finance-uae` skill, including its regulatory, API and commercial references, then checked public sources on 14 September 2026:

- [CBUAE Open Finance licensing provisions](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/entiresection/6127): distinguish legal status, permitted activities and the applicable licensing/approval route; no assumption of a licence from the institution name.
- [CBUAE Article 4 activity limitations](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/article-4-limitations-0): review boundaries around advice and customer funds; payment initiation is not represented as Meridian holding or executing funds.
- [UAE v2.1 consent guidance](https://www.nebras-open-finance.com/tech/tpp-standards/v2.1/consent): distinct data/service consent scopes, API Hub consent authority and bank-side customer authentication.
- [TPP integration guide overview](https://nebras-open-finance.com/tech/tpp-standards): Trust Framework, security, banking capabilities, consent, events and certification areas. This community guide is not an official CBUAE publication. Its unversioned route currently displays v2.2-rc1, so no production-version claim is derived from it.

The page links domain references and asks adopters to verify actual requirements and deployed capabilities. It quotes no fee, capital, SLA, deadline, schema field or production-version promise. It is a worked method illustration, not a complete compliance assessment or implementation specification.
